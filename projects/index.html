RawThoughts

RawThoughts is where I try ideas in software and write down what the work taught me. Pale and Gazette grew there. It is also RTEPL’s engineering and consulting practice. Causality and Post-AGI Economics follow related questions about intelligence and the economy.

Causality

I explore how intelligence changes its understanding through mathematical descriptions, adaptive reasoning units and collective architectures, connecting representation, memory and learning.

Post-AGI Economics

I study how cheaper intelligence changes production constraints, ownership and household income, and whether the gains reach the people who need to buy what is produced.

Sleepwalker

I am developing a mythic science-fiction graphic novel about seven children, an impossible book and dreams that awaken ruins on distant worlds. Vedic inquiry and studies in image, language and sequence explore the questions and craft behind it.

DAO Studio

I co-founded DAO Studio in 2022 with the ambition of helping people create decentralised autonomous organisations. The work developed into coordinated emergency response with YOJN Care, inspectable AI applications with YojnAI, and DAOnet’s design for carrying reusable capability between teams.

Choosing a place to start

Are these all parts of one project?

They share one question: how a person, a model, an organisation or a society with limited knowledge and resources forms a workable picture of reality, acts through it, learns from what follows and keeps agency and accountability as scale and capability grow. The home page asks it in three forms: how does understanding become useful, what should a working system remember, and what can a story let us see that a model misses? Each home follows it in a different way: Causality as mathematics, Post-AGI Economics as economics and Sleepwalker as myth, with RawThoughts where the answers get built and used. A problem in a working system can become a research question; a mathematical idea can suggest a story; an economic change can alter what is worth building. Each needs room to develop its own argument and show what it has actually found.

Where should I start?

RawThoughts follows useful engineering and collaboration, and its products are the place to see that work as one system. Causality takes the formal questions, Post-AGI Economics follows economic consequences, and Sleepwalker opens into a graphic novel and the inquiry and creative work behind it. DAO Studio brings together coordinated response, inspectable AI applications and reusable capability. Product and implementation enquiries go through RawThoughts’ services.
