Title: Building products and teams

[Read HTML](https://getharsh.in/collections/building-products-and-teams/)

Author: Harsh Joshi

A product changes the work around it. Somebody has to install it, make sense of its output, handle an exception and keep it useful after the people who built it have moved on. I learned that through physical equipment before encountering it in customer workflows and AI applications. The component was often the part I knew how to build first. Understanding the rest could change what the component ought to be.

The work also changed me. Building equipment drew me into the operator's day; leading development drew me into other people's judgment. Later, founding, consulting and research gave me different ways to contribute, and different limits on what a useful contribution could carry. Some of those changes need an ordered account. Others began with a particular problem that still deserves its own argument.
