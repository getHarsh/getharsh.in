Title: Energy efficiency

[Read HTML](https://getharsh.in/collections/energy-efficiency/)

When I joined Smart Joules as its founding engineer in 2016, the brief was short: commercial buildings were doubling every decade, around 40% of their energy was wasted, fix it. I knew nothing about energy efficiency. So I went to a hospital, got permission to experiment with its cooling plant, read the machines over Modbus and plotted what they were doing.

Two things became hard to ignore. Most of the waste sat in the HVAC system, and the operators running it were pressing red and green buttons with no way to see what those buttons did to the plant. The product thesis grew from there: make the controls simple enough for the people on shift, and use what they do as the context for optimising the plant.

These pieces follow that thesis in order: what a saving must preserve, whether a command reached the equipment, and when advice actually helps the operator.
